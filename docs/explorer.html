How to read this

Bounce rate, lower is better. Above 2% on a domain usually means it needs pausing, warming up again, or a sending-reputation check. Reply rate, higher is better. Below 1% often points to targeting or copy that needs a refresh. Use Week From / Week To to pick a time window, search a domain or mailbox to isolate it, or set numeric ranges to surface outliers (e.g. bounce % min 3 finds every domain currently in trouble). The two stat boxes below always show your currently selected range on the left, with the latest available week and the week before it (both fixed reference points, not tied to your filter selection) alongside for quick comparison. Switch between Domain and Mailbox view below to change how granular the table gets.
